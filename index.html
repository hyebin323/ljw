About the model

Built with EfficientNet-B0

A transfer-learning approach combining pretrained ImageNet features with a custom classification head fine-tuned on bone fracture radiographs.

01 / ARCHITECTURE

EfficientNet-B0

Frozen convolutional backbone pretrained on ImageNet, topped with global average pooling, batch normalization, a 256-unit dense layer, and a softmax classifier.

02 / TRAINING

Transfer learning

Trained on Google Colab T4 GPU. Single-stage training with class weighting, horizontal flip, rotation, zoom and contrast augmentation. Early stopping with patience of 3 epochs.

03 / INPUT

224 × 224 RGB

Images are resized to 224 pixels, normalized using the EfficientNet preprocessing pipeline, and fed as a single-sample batch to the model for inference.

04 / OUTPUT

Six-class softmax

The model outputs a probability distribution over six consolidated fracture categories derived from a ten-class medical taxonomy.

Six diagnostic categories

Fracture Taxonomy

Ten medical fracture types consolidated into six clinically meaningful groups to improve classifier reliability on limited training data.

Complete Comminuted · Oblique · Spiral · Longitudinal
Incomplete Greenstick · Hairline
Avulsion Bone fragment pulled by tendon
Impacted Bone driven into itself
Pathological Fracture from underlying disease
Dislocation Fracture with joint dislocation
